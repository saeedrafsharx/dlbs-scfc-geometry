- dlbs_pipeline_v3.py
- diagnose_qc.py

dlbs_pipeline_v3.py is a from-scratch rewrite of the Stage 1 structural/functional
connectome pipeline, replacing the affine-atlas-resampling approach in
run_coupling_potential.py (kept for reference as the original v2 pipeline) with
ANTsPy-based nonlinear (SyN) registration, corrected transform inversion, and
numerous correctness, speed, and quality fixes documented in the module
docstring. diagnose_qc.py and check_reproducibility.py are quality-control
utilities that operate on dlbs_pipeline_v3.py's outputs. Hard-coded Kaggle
paths in all three were replaced with the repository's existing
environment-variable/relative-path convention; behavior is otherwise
unchanged from the uploaded source.
